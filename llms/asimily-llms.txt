# Asimily

> Next-Generation Cyber Asset and Exposure Management Platform for IoT, OT, IT, and IoMT

## Company Overview

Asimily provides a comprehensive cybersecurity platform designed specifically for protecting connected devices across IoT (Internet of Things), OT (Operational Technology), IT, and IoMT (Internet of Medical Things) environments. Founded in 2017 by Shankar Somasundaram after his experience leading IoT security at Symantec where he secured over a billion IoT devices, Asimily addresses the unique security challenges of connected devices and mission-critical systems that traditional IT security solutions fail to adequately protect.

The platform recognizes that every connected device represents a potential attack vector, and that IoT, OT, and IoMT devices have unique constraints that require specialized security approaches—they cannot be secured using traditional IT security methods alone.

Asimily differentiates itself through:

1. **Patented AI/ML Analysis** - Deep vulnerability analysis beyond basic categorization, with MITRE ATT&CK-based risk assessment specific to each device's context

2. **Multiple Mitigation Tactics** - Not just discovery, but actionable risk reduction through:
   - Network segmentation and microsegmentation

   - Hybrid approaches tailored to environment

## Company Philosophy

Asimily operates on core principles that recognize the unique challenges of IoT/OT security:

1. **Risk Reduction Over Vulnerability Lists** - You can't close every vulnerability, but you can systematically reduce real business risk across IT, IoT, OT, and IoMT devices

2. **Context-Driven Security** - Actionable insight requires more than data—it demands understanding each device's role, environment, and constraints combined with advanced technology

3. **Multiple Mitigation Strategies** - Organizations need diverse tactics and workflows tailored to specific devices and contexts to reduce risk quickly, not one-size-fits-all approaches

4. **Complete Lifecycle Management** - Effective security spans from pre-purchase risk assessment through operational monitoring to decommissioning—at a fair price

5. **Protection Without Disruption** - Security must work with the constraints of IoT/OT devices: legacy systems, mission-critical operations, and devices that cannot tolerate downtime or active scanning
